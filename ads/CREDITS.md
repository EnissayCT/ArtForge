## Other visual elements

- **Brand mark:** redrawn from `src/components/brand/Logo.tsx` (the brand's own logo).
- **Brushed-metal texture** (`public/tex/brushed.png`): generated with ffmpeg (`noise` + directional `gblur`). Original.
- **Phones and the printed case:** drawn in CSS, no photos or vendor artwork. The case uses the iPhone 16 Pro proportions from the site's `src/lib/phones.ts`. "iPhone", "Samsung" and "Xiaomi" appear only as text, to name the compatible phones. They are trademarks of their owners, and no logos are shown.
- **Paper grain:** an SVG `feTurbulence` filter, the same as the site's `.grain`.
